🏃 Activity
  • Walk 5–10 minutes each day. Increase slowly.
  • Avoid lifting anything heavier than 10 pounds.
  • Do not drive until your doctor says it is okay.
  • Rest when you feel tired. Sleep 7–8 hours each night.
🍽️ Diet
  • Follow a low-sodium (low-salt) diet.
  • Limit fluids to about 2 litres per day unless told otherwise.
  • Weigh yourself every morning. Call if you gain 2 pounds in 1 day.
🚨 Go to ER Now
  • Chest pain or tightness
  • Severe shortness of breath
  • Fainting or severe dizziness
  • Coughing up blood
